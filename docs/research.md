[Leapfin pricing](https://www.leapfin.com/pricing) lists Standard at $500/month, Premium at $1,000/month and Enterprise with a custom price. Its FAQ separately describes a $1,000 monthly base. Use the explicit Premium label, not a claim that every customer pays that amount. Twelve Premium months total $12,000. The page shows a dollar symbol without an explicit currency code; confirm currency in a vendor quote. This is public-price arithmetic, not a customer invoice. Its FAQ says subscriptions are annual. The page also announces Leapfin joining Airwallex; the product and documentation remained accessible this run.

[Maxio pricing](https://www.maxio.com/pricing) lists Grow at $599/month, with Scale quoted privately. No actual NZ or AU customer invoice was verified for either vendor. Repository context search did not establish an EDNA buyer; the buyer score remains 2. No live customer database was accessed.

Search results contain [G2's Leapfin alternatives page](https://www.g2.com/products/leapfin/competitors/alternatives) and comparison listicles. This is a weak search-demand proxy, not measured volume. No traffic or savings claim is made.

Leapfin's [record filtering documentation](https://leapfin.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/PD/pages/164331575/View+and+filter+your+accounting+records) states that accounting record filters cannot search through links to other records. The ten questions here join contracts, schedules, imported records, notes and approvals. Leapfin also has warehouse reporting, so the claim is about that record-search screen, not the impossibility of answering them elsewhere in Leapfin.

The export mapping and accounting references are in replace-leapfin.md and compliance.md. Native input fixtures use documented fields and fictional values. Nothing here is a captured customer export. The build does not replace all of Leapfin.
